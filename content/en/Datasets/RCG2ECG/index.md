[MMECG](https://github.com/jinbochen0823/RCG2ECG) is an open-source dataset of 10 hours of processed mmWave radar data and synchronized ECG measurements collected from 35 participants under four different physiological statuses. This dataset is used in the paper [Contactless Electrocardiogram Monitoring With Millimeter Wave Radar](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/9919401). It can facilitate digital health research based on mmWave radar. (RightNow, we only release 4.55 hours of data. The rest of the data are still under the authorizing process with privacy concern. We will update it once we finish the authorizing process.)
